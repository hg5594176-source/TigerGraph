## Option 1: LinkedIn (In-depth Technical Summary)

🚀 Excited to unveil our project for the **HHGOA Agentic Fraud Investigation Hackathon** built on @TigerGraph!

Investigating modern financial fraud isn't a simple binary classification problem—it requires connecting obscured dots across accounts, transaction velocities, device fingerprints, and regulatory policies.

We engineered an autonomous Agentic Fraud Investigator combining:
⚡ **TigerGraph Savanna**: High-speed graph queries (BFS ring discovery, PageRank, structural precedent filtering) across 590k IEEE-CIS transactions.
🧠 **TigerVector & GraphRAG**: Semantic retrieval of bank compliance policies and past case narratives.
🔄 **LangGraph Multi-Hypothesis Engine**: A 10-node state machine that iteratively collects evidence, evaluates competing hypotheses, and halts on deterministic mathematical stopping rules.
🛡️ **Deterministic Policy Layer**: Strict governance that prevents LLM prompt injections and enforces mandatory human approval roles (Senior Analyst, Compliance Officer) before sensitive actions (Account Freeze, SAR filings) can execute.
📊 **Interactive Analyst Dashboard**: Real-time D3.js force-directed subgraph visualization, hypothesis meters, and chronological audit timelines.

Special thanks to @TigerGraphDB and @247pmstudio for organizing an incredible hackathon pushing the boundaries of Graph + Agentic AI!

Meet our autonomous fraud investigation agent powered by #TigerGraph Savanna, TigerVector & #LangGraph. Here is how it works 🧵👇

2/5 🕸️ **Graph-First Intelligence**: We reconstructed 13.5k pseudo-identities from 590k IEEE-CIS transactions. 5 custom GSQL queries traverse multi-hop device & email rings in milliseconds, uncovering coordinated fraud networks that tabular models miss.

3/5 🎯 **Multi-Hypothesis Reasoning**: Rather than rushing to judgment, the agent maintains an active blackboard scoring 5 fraud typologies simultaneously, backed by a deterministic non-LLM stopping rule to avoid infinite loops and hallucinations.

4/5 🛡️ **Zero-Trust Security Layer**: The LLM proposes, but our deterministic permission layer disposes. High-impact actions (Account Freeze, SAR Filings) strictly require human sign-off (Senior Analyst / Compliance Officer). Prompt injection immune!
